STUDENTHUB

General Questions

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a centralized student portal that provides access to academic information, announcements, events, student profiles and other college services.

Who can use StudentHub?

Students, teachers, faculty members and authorized college administrators can use StudentHub according to their assigned roles.

How do I create a StudentHub account?

Visit the Registration page and enter your required student information. After completing registration, you can use your account to access the StudentHub portal.

Is StudentHub available on mobile devices?

Yes. StudentHub is designed using responsive layouts so that students can access the portal from desktops, tablets and mobile devices.

ACADEMICS

Academic Questions

Where can I view my academic information?

You can view your academic information from your Dashboard or Profile page after logging into StudentHub.

Where can I find assignments?

Available assignments can be accessed through the academic Dashboard. Your teachers can upload new assignments and provide submission details.

Can I check my attendance?

Yes. Attendance information can be displayed on your Dashboard when it has been updated by the respective faculty member.

Where can I find college announcements?

Important announcements can be displayed on the StudentHub Home page and Dashboard.

CAMPUS LIFE

Events & Activities

Where can I see upcoming college events?

Visit the Events page to view upcoming technical, cultural, sports and other college activities.

How can I register for an event?

Open the Events page, select the event you are interested in and use the registration option provided for that event.

Can students participate in multiple events?

Students can participate in multiple events depending on the event schedule and the registration requirements.

ACCOUNT

Account & Support

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the password recovery option on the Login page. If you still cannot access your account, contact the StudentHub support team.

How can I update my profile information?

Open your Profile page and select the Edit Profile option to update information that you are allowed to change.

How can I contact StudentHub support?

Visit the Contact page to find the support email address, phone number and department contact information.
