General Questions

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is an online academic portal for students and faculty.

2. How can I register?

Go to the Register page and complete the registration form.

3. How do I view attendance?

Login to your account and open the Dashboard page.

4. Where can I download study materials?

Study materials are available in the student dashboard after login.

5. Who can upload assignments?

Only faculty members and administrators can upload assignments.
