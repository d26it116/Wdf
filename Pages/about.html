Who We Are

StudentHub is an online academic management portal developed for students and faculty to access important academic information from one place.

Objectives

  • Manage student records.
  • Share announcements.
  • Upload assignments.
  • Provide study materials.
  • Track attendance.
  • Improve communication.

Our Vision

To build a smart, secure and user-friendly academic platform.
